MARLY Creative Concepts

Animated HTML5 display, built like a system.

Marly is the solo studio I founded and run: a Figma-to-production pipeline that turns brand design systems into animated ad sets. I’m the creative director, the pipeline architect, and the final QA gate, which is why the system below exists at all.

Enterprise platforms like Celtra automate at scale but assume someone else is producing the creative. Agencies charge $350–1,500 per ad set and reset the file handoff with every campaign. DIY tools produce output that looks like Canva, because it is.

D2C brands spend real money on type, palette, and a product with a shape worth showing, and still can’t get those choices honored downstream.
